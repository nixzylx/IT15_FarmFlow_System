# FarmFlow - Agricultural ERP & CRM System

🌾 A comprehensive ERP and CRM system designed for small to medium-sized agricultural businesses.

---

## 📋 Overview

FarmFlow is an integrated ERP and CRM system that streamlines core agricultural business operations — including farm production, crop management, inventory, purchasing, and sales — into a single, unified platform.

### 🎯 Target Users

- **Farm Owners/Administrators** - Full system oversight and strategic decision-making
- **Production Managers** - Planting/harvesting schedules and field staff management
- **Warehouse Keepers** - Stock management, transfers, and wastage tracking
- **Procurement Officers** - Purchase orders and supplier management
- **Sales Staff/Agents** - Order management and customer relationship management
- **Accounting Staff** - Expense tracking and financial reporting

---

## 🚀 Features

### Core Modules
- ✅ **Authentication & Authorization** - Secure login with role-based access
- 🔄 **Farm Production Management** - Crop planning, batch tracking, and harvest management
- 🔄 **Inventory Management** - Stock control, transfers, and wastage tracking
- 🔄 **Purchasing Management** - Purchase orders, requisitions, and supplier management
- 🔄 **Sales & CRM** - Sales orders, quotations, and customer relationship management
- 🔄 **Expense Tracking** - Financial tracking and payment processing
- 🔄 **Reports & Analytics** - Comprehensive reporting and dashboards

---

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

- **Backend:** ASP.NET Core MVC (C#)
- **Database:** SQL Server with Entity Framework Core
- **Frontend:** HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, Bootstrap
- **Authentication:** Cookie-based authentication
- **Deployment:** IIS / Azure / Docker
